## 1. Published Research Paper

### "Bias Detection and Mitigation in Large Language Models: A Fairness-Driven Approach"
- **Publisher:** Taylor & Francis (CRC Press)
- **Year of Publication:** 2025
- **Authors:** Suyash Shukla, et al.
- **Subject Areas:** Natural Language Processing, Algorithmic Fairness, Ethical AI, Large Language Models
- **Abstract & Focus:**
  Contemporary autoregressive large language models exhibit non-trivial demographic, cultural, and representational disparities inherited from uncurated pre-training corpora. This research formalizes a multi-dimensional evaluation benchmark evaluating stereotyping and disparity across protected demographic groups. It introduces a post-hoc counterfactual mitigation strategy that adjusts generation logits at inference time without requiring expensive full-model retraining, preserving downstream task accuracy while reducing disparate toxicity and stereotyping scores.
