### What is EarnBuddy?
EarnBuddy (https://earnbuddy.io) is a microservices SaaS freelance and task marketplace platform co-founded by Suyash Shukla. ### What is Caller.work?
Caller.work (https://caller.work) is a fault-tolerant, webhook-driven telephony backend designed to reliably trigger, monitor, and retry outbound automated calls without dropped notifications under high concurrency, achieving 99.95% deliverability across 50,000+ calls per month.

### What is Prayukti?
Prayukti (https://mmmut.prayukti.org) is an interactive virtual laboratory platform built to provide engineering and science students with browser-based access to laboratory experiments. Over 400 students across Indian engineering colleges use Prayukti to simulate experiments, collect real-time data, and submit graded lab records.
